NumSharp.Interop.OpenBLAS itself is licensed Apache-2.0, like the rest of NumSharp.

This package additionally REDISTRIBUTES prebuilt OpenBLAS shared libraries as
NuGet runtime assets (runtimes/<rid>/native/). They are the unmodified artifacts
published on PyPI by the openblas-libs project as 0.3.34.106.0
-- the very same binaries NumPy 2.5.4 bundles in its own wheels, which is what makes
NumSharp's matrix products bit-identical to that NumPy's.
