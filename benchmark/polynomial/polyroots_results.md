# numpy.polynomial companion matrices and roots (U7) — NumSharp vs NumPy 2.4.2

Generated by `benchmark/polynomial/polyroots_report.py` from a `polyroots_bench.cs` run. **NPY/NS = NumPy time /
NumSharp time — higher is better, > 1 means NumSharp is faster.** Every cell's result was checked against
NumPy's before it was timed: 229 of 229 cells are bit-exact (SHA-256 of dtype, shape
and data; a float32 series' complex roots compared as NumPy's complex64 values widened to complex128, the
values NumSharp carries).
Roots of degree 2 or more are `np.linalg.eigvals` of the companion matrix — LAPACK `geev` on both sides,
NumSharp through `NumSharp.Interop.OpenBLAS` (the scipy-openblas NumPy loads) at one thread: as the degree
grows, both sides converge on the same `geev` call, the physical ceiling of the larger roots cells.

Method: both sides pinned to the same 4 logical CPUs (`NS_PROBE_AFFINITY=0xF0`), never concurrently; C# in
Release with `DOTNET_TC_CallCountingDelayMs=0` and a drained tier-1 warm-up (warm, sleep, warm again);
best-of-rounds per side (rounds sized from one timed NumPy call); inputs from one exact formula both languages
evaluate (no arrays on disk); `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1` on the NumPy side.
Host: Intel64 Family 6 Model 183 Stepping 1, GenuineIntel, Windows 11.
