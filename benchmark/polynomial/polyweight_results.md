NumSharp time — higher is better, > 1 means NumSharp is faster.** Every cell's result was compared byte for

with `DOTNET_TC_CallCountingDelayMs=0` and a drained tier-1 warm-up (warm, sleep, warm again); best-of-rounds
per side; inputs from one exact formula both languages evaluate (no arrays on disk).
