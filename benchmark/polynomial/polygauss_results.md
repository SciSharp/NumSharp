# numpy.polynomial Gauss-Legendre / Gauss-Laguerre quadrature (U8) — NumSharp vs NumPy 2.4.2

Generated by `benchmark/polynomial/polygauss_report.py` from a `polygauss_bench.cs` run. **NPY/NS = NumPy time /
NumSharp time — higher is better, > 1 means NumSharp is faster.** Every cell's two result slots were compared byte
for byte (SHA-256 of dtype, shape and data) against NumPy's before it was timed: all 22 cells are bit-exact.

Method: both sides pinned to the same two P-cores (`NS_PROBE_AFFINITY=0x14`), never concurrently, OpenBLAS at ONE
thread on both sides (NumPy under `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`, NumSharp through `NumSharp.Interop.OpenBLAS`, the same
scipy-openblas build); C# in Release with `DOTNET_TC_CallCountingDelayMs=0` and a drained tier-1 warm-up;
best-of-rounds per side. NumPy's time at low degrees is its three `{p}val` calls, Python loops over the deg + 1
coefficients; from degree ~100 both sides wait on the same LAPACK `syevd`, so the ratio falls toward 1 there.
Host: Intel64 Family 6 Model 183 Stepping 1, GenuineIntel, Windows 11.
