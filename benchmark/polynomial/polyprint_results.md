Where the time goes. A unique request whose cutoff does not cut the shortest digits is Ryu's shortest digits (the
fast path), 0.1-0.3 us. Every other request runs NumPy's Dragon4 loop on NumPy's own in-place BigInt arithmetic,
ported with 64-bit blocks (Dragon4.BigInt.cs): no allocation, half NumPy's passes per digit, and two exact
shortcuts NumPy's loop does not take — margins dropped once they pass the scale (min_digits past the shortest
digits), and 19 digits per division where the margins no longer matter (long exact runs: 1e300 to every place).
NumPy's side adds its Python wrapper (about 0.6 us of argument checks around the C call); format_float adds
np.get_printoptions and NumPy-scalar arithmetic (3-5 us), and set_default_printstyle a tuple membership test.
